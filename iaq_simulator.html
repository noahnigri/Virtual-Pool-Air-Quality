IAQ Simulator — Indoor Air Quality Model

Generic 6-lane 25-yd training pool · Base model for further development

Lagrangian particle simulation of supply-to-return airflow circulation. Particles are emitted at HVAC supply diffusers, travel through the velocity field (with thermal buoyancy over the warm pool), and are absorbed at return vents.
